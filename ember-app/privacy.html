Ember for iPhone · Privacy policy

Nothing leaves the fire.

Ember doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone

The names you type, the cards you write, and where you are in the night are saved on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

What Ember doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No network connections: Ember works entirely offline.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Ember collects no information from anyone, including children.
