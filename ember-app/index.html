Ember

One phone, passed hand to hand. The questions start light and go deeper as the fire burns down.

How to play

  • Type everyone's names in the order you're sitting. The phone always moves to the left.
  • Pick how you know each other (or none, for a mix), and switch on Kids around the fire if you need every question to be all-ages.
  • Tap the fire. A spark flies up and becomes a card. Answer it, or everyone does, then pass the phone on. Don't like a card? Burn it.
  • Secret ballots go around hidden, one person at a time, then the votes fly out of the fire.
  • The menu (top right) sets how long the night runs, how deep the questions go, and lets you write your own cards.

Ember collects nothing. It works offline, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and nothing you type ever leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy.
